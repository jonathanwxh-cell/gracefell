# GRACEFELL v2.27.4 release record — room to read

Date: 2026-07-29

Implementation and verification: Codex (GPT-5 / Codex Desktop)

Planning and play review: six role-directed agents

Direction: [@jonathanwxh-cell](https://github.com/jonathanwxh-cell)

Package: `gracefell@2.27.4`

## Outcome

Gracefell already had the right compact combat vocabulary. The first-run
problem was timing: the 2.6-second intro consumed most of a 4.5-second tutorial
message, Malakar could select an attack after only 0.4 seconds of playable
time, and the faint touch movement target could disappear before movement was
learned. Two independent baseline phone roles scored the result below 8/10.

v2.27.4 gives a new Journey player room to read and act while leaving the
established game intact. It adds no mechanic, button, mode, boss pattern,
reward, currency, or permanent HUD.

## Research boundary

The change follows three current principles:

- [Apple game controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-controls)
  recommends reachable, large, responsive touch controls and a predictable
  movement region.
- [Apple Designing for games](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-games/)
  recommends teaching through play and helping players feel successful quickly.
- [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 116](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/116)
  asks games to give players enough time to read, interpret, and act on
  important UI.
- [Boss Up: Boss Battle Design Fundamentals and Retro](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2018/presentations/Keren_Itay_BossUp.pdf)
  emphasizes clear goals, visible progress, and avoiding helplessness.

Those sources supported a timing and truthfulness fix. They did not justify a
new tutorial screen, permanent panel, extra action, or easier global balance.

## Implementation

- `openingStalkSeconds()` returns 3.2 seconds only when the tutorial is
  unfinished and the selected path is a negative Journey. Tutorial-complete,
  Measured, and Oath runs retain 0.4 seconds.
- `tutorialT` no longer counts down during the intro. The MOVE lesson receives
  its complete 4.5 seconds after the fight becomes controllable.
- The existing faint touch MOVE target remains visible while the MOVE lesson
  is active, including when another action briefly shortens the generic hint.
- The existing ROLL lesson lasts four seconds after first movement instead of
  six, reducing close-range banner competition.
- Semantic Combat tips are split into Roll slash, Sunder, and Gracebreak.
  Touch says to tap Break; desktop says to hold Heavy through the charge.
- The phone and README descriptions now match the v2.27.3 tap-driven BREAK
  behavior.

### Frozen-diff review hardening

The first PR-head review found that the MOVE ring still depended on
`tutorialT > 0`. A player who waited longer than 4.5 seconds without moving
would therefore lose both the text and spatial affordance while
`tutorialStage` still said `move`.

The final candidate keeps a restrained 0.19-opacity MOVE ring whenever the
MOVE stage remains unlearned, then removes that persistent affordance as soon
as movement advances the stage. True-touch QA now renders the post-expiry
state and fails unless `MOVE` remains present before learning and absent
afterward.

## Unchanged contract

- No damage, poise, stamina, Resolve, health, iframe, telegraph, or recovery
  value changed.
- No boss action, phase, chain, difficulty modifier, score, save field, audio
  event, asset, touch target, camera, or render budget changed.
- Sunder, Execute, Gracebreak, roll slash, and input priority are unchanged.
- Returning players, Measured, and every Oath keep the established 0.4-second
  opening.

## Five final gamer-role scorecards

These are independent agent simulations of specified gamer roles, not claims
of a recruited human panel. The first exact-candidate pass scored 8.0–8.6.
After the frozen-diff finding changed the bundle, all five roles reran so the
final threshold rests on the amended candidate rather than inferred parity.

| Role | Score | Key evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Accessibility and reduced-motion player | 8.0/10 | 360×640 DPR2 true touch; left-hand/reduced-effect settings; guide survived 4.77 idle seconds and cleared after a 263-unit drag |
| Distracted five-minute player | 8.4/10 | 2.83 s launch-to-playable; pause froze state/boss timers; touch Sunder worked; guide left combat after movement |
| Experienced touch regular | 8.9/10 | 360×640 DPR2 pressure play; two staggers and 371 damage; persistent ring stayed restrained and cleared after movement |
| Mobile action newcomer | 9.0/10 | 390×844 DPR2; guide present at 4.85 s with timers zero; all five touch verbs worked; 183 damage in the next 18.5 s |
| Souls/boss-game veteran | 9.0/10 | First windup 3.48 s, damage 4.26 s; stagger at 5.88 s; tutorial done at 6.90 s; 357 damage in 18.4 s |

Every role had no zero category and no observed control blocker. Remaining
minor deductions were close-range silhouette compression, initial HUD density,
bounded offensive/end-state sampling in some roles, and headless limits around
physical haptics, thumb fatigue, and valid cross-tab visibility events.

## Known limits

- Headless touch validates geometry, state, screenshots, and event ownership;
  subjective haptics, thumb fatigue, and phone-speaker mix still need a
  physical device.
- The existing Browserslist-age and optional Three.js large-chunk warnings are
  unchanged.
- Dependencies and save schema are unchanged.

## Rollback

Revert the reviewed v2.27.4 runtime merge, run the complete gate, deploy the
reviewed revert, restart `gracefell.service`, and rerun public QA. No migration
is required.
