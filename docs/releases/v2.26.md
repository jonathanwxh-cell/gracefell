## Outcome

The v2.22 recorded-foley foundation already names the important actions. v2.26
finishes the consequence layer around those actions so the sound arrives at the
moment and severity the player sees:

- a heavy swing speaks on the actual strike frame, with normalized charge
  shaping the same release and contact families;
- adjusted incoming damage selects light, medium, or heavy hurt treatment;
- a hostile projectile owns at most one safe closest-pass cue and a damaging
  hit can never also claim it;
- insufficient stamina produces a restrained warning without eating the
  established buffered command;
- the arena ward speaks once on a meaningful outward collision and remains
  latched during boundary sliding;
- the victory grade seal stamps once at its established reveal.

This release adds no audio file. `SFX_MANIFEST` remains exactly 45 accepted MP3s,
so `SFX_VERSION` and the asset hashes do not change.

## Event ownership

### Charged heavy

The old heavy sample started when HVY was accepted, about 420 ms before the
active frame. The same sample now starts when the blade releases. A quick tap
passes charge `0`; a full hold passes `1`.

Charge continuously and conservatively adjusts sample playback rate, gain,
room send, phone transient, procedural body, sub duration, and resonance. It
does not add a second full release or impact. The sustained charge voice keeps
one owner, fades to silence in 90 ms on release, and stops immediately when
damage interrupts it.

Damage, poise, stamina cost, root time, lunge, range, hit-stop, hit geometry, and
charge multiplier are unchanged.

### Three hurt tiers

`Player.takeDamage` now passes the final adjusted damage rather than a boolean:

| Adjusted damage | Tier | Treatment |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| `1..12` | light | higher, shorter, least ducking |
| `13..20` | medium | existing heavy body at neutral pitch and bounded weight |
| `21+` | heavy | lower body, stronger duck, one delayed sub tail |

All tiers retain a phone-safe contact edge. The heavy tier adds one delayed
tone, not another recording or unbounded layer.

### Closest pass

Each hostile projectile stores only two optional numbers/flags. Its closest
player distance is updated during existing projectile movement. A cue fires
once when the projectile has begun receding and its closest sampled distance
was:

- more than two pixels outside combined contact radius; and
- no more than 44 pixels beyond combined contact radius.

Collision is checked first. Any collision attempt marks near-miss ownership as
resolved, so a hit or invulnerable overlap cannot later create a second whoosh.
Projectile speed, radius, damage, lifetime, allocation count, and collision are
unchanged.

### Stamina denial and ward contact

A buffered roll, light, or heavy request below its existing cost calls a
350-ms-gated two-voice procedural warning. The command remains in the 260 ms
input buffer and can still execute if regeneration reaches the cost. No
transition priority or regeneration value changes.

`clampArena` now reports whether it clamped while preserving the same position
math. The player calls the existing ward family only on the first frame of an
outward collision above 70 px/s. A latch prevents repeated chimes while sliding
against the boundary; moving away rearms it. Boss clamping ignores the return
value and remains silent.

### Grade seal

The terminal result calls `gradeStamp(grade)` once when `stateT` crosses the
existing 1.5-second visual reveal. It reuses the accepted stamp recording. An S
adds one restrained ascending tone. Result timing, scoring, persistence, the
4.5-second input hold, and replay controls are unchanged.

### Cross-platform phase-score finalization

The first clean GitHub Linux runner exposed one existing edge while pausing and
resuming the Phase 2 crossfade. Chromium entered the finish branch, then
reported its next `currentTime` fractionally inside the 720 ms value curve and
rejected a final `setValueAtTime` as overlapping.

Endpoint cleanup now schedules 5 ms beyond both the authored curve end and the
observed audio clock. The equal-power curves already hold their final values,
so the margin is inaudible and does not lengthen the transition. It only avoids
an illegal same-quantum automation write. The normal phase, pause, retry,
fixed-node-count, and console-clean assertions remain the regression contract.

## Performance boundary

The release adds:

- no MP3, image, font, GLB, network request, loader worker, or cache version;
- no startup Web Audio node or decoded buffer;
- no render operation, particle, surface, animation loop, or dependency;
- no save field or migration.

New work occurs only on existing action, damage, collision, clamp, projectile,
or terminal frames. All sound still routes through `GameAudio` voice
reservation, spatial processing, room send, music ducking, and the established
limiter.

## Deterministic acceptance

`qa/verify.cjs` now proves in real mobile Chromium:

- quick and full heavy releases arrive once with charge `0` and `1`;
- boss contact receives the same normalized charge;
- damage `10`, `16`, and `24` maps to light, medium, and heavy;
- a low-stamina heavy warning occurs once while the heavy command stays
  buffered and the player stays in `move`;
- an outward ward collision clamps exactly, chimes once, and remains latched;
- a safe projectile pass voices once across repeated frames;
- a damaging projectile is removed and does not add a near-miss;
- the S seal stamps exactly once after 1.5 seconds;
- recorded release/contact rates become lower with charge and the three hurt
  sample profiles remain distinct;
- the existing sustained charge voice still deduplicates and cleans up on
  release, damage, reset, title return, and destruction.

The existing complete desktop, 390×844 mobile, true-touch, mastery, v2.24
impact-frame, deterministic render-cost, v2.25 visual/failure, static-asset,
save, difficulty, pause, score, and public replay lanes remain mandatory.
