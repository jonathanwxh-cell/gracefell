# GRACEFELL v2.27 release record — earned offense

Date: 2026-07-27
Implementation and verification: Codex (GPT-5 / Codex Desktop)
Direction: [@jonathanwxh-cell](https://github.com/jonathanwxh-cell)
Package: `gracefell@2.27.0`

## Outcome

v2.27 adds one mixed combo and one earned limit release without adding a
permanent action button, asset family, persistent progression system, or new
render surface:

- two connected light hits followed by HVY branch into **Sunder**;
- successful and varied combat fills a 100-point run-local **Resolve** meter;
- full Resolve plus a fully charged HVY releases **Gracebreak**;
- the existing HUD, touch HVY, chain lane, semantic companion, and bounded
  hybrid audio graph communicate those states;
- save schema v7, score/path identity, boss patterns, defensive timing, assets,
  music, and four-button touch geometry remain intact.

This is a compact offense layer around the established poise/Execute loop, not
a replacement for it.

## Authored combat contract

### Sunder

`ATK · ATK · HVY` becomes Sunder only after both light attacks connect.
Repeated presses do not qualify. The contact route is separate from the
presentation-only light-chain counter.

| Route | Damage | Poise | Stamina |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `ATK · ATK · ATK` | 50 | 50 | 36 |
| `ATK · ATK · HVY` | 50 | 70 | 44 |

The Sunder ender is 24 damage, 44 total poise, 20 stamina, a 480 ms action, and
a 600 ms decision window after the second contact. Roll has transition
priority. A missed first/second light, damage, expiry, or defensive cancel
clears the route. This makes Sunder a deliberate poise trade rather than a
strict replacement for the efficient light finisher.

### Resolve

Resolve is capped at 100 and resets with every new or abandoned fight.

| Connected event | Gain |
|---|---:|
| light 1 / light 2 / light finisher | 1 / 1 / 3 |
| Sunder | 6 |
| roll slash | 4 |
| perfect dodge | 8 |
| heavy charged to at least 70% | 6 |
| flank | 4 |
| Execute | 12 |

Flank adds to the base event. Gains are awarded only after contact and only
while Malakar remains alive. Resolve is absent from save-v7, victory scores,
best-time partitions, grade calculation, and path records.

Journey −2 and −3 receive 4 Resolve for a non-lethal wound, capped at 12 for
the attempt. Journey −1, Measured, and every Oath receive zero from wounds.

### Gracebreak

At 100 Resolve, a fully charged existing HVY becomes Gracebreak:

- 72 damage;
- 112 total poise;
- meter spent at the release frame, including on a miss;
- 20 stamina restored only after contact;
- no invulnerability, hazard clearing, teleport, or forced boss interrupt;
- partial/quick heavy preserves the full meter;
- staggered Execute takes priority and preserves the meter;
- Oath +5 remains IRONBOUND and cannot be staggered.

The whiff rule and lack of emergency defense keep the release consequential.
The Execute priority preserves the defensive mastery loop that defines the
fight.

## Interface and accessibility

- A four-segment Resolve rail sits between stamina and flasks inside the
  protected `playerHudRect()`.
- Full Resolve changes the existing touch HVY label to `BREAK` and adds one
  restrained spirit outline; there is no fifth touch target.
- Sunder availability and technique results reuse the established chain lane
  above the combat centerline.
- `Game.uiSnapshot().combat` exposes `resolvePercent`, `resolveReady`, and
  `technique`.
- The semantic game-controls panel names Resolve and the current technique
  without turning 250 ms numeric refreshes into live-announcement spam.
- The combat tips teach two-light Sunder and full-Resolve hold-HVY.

The 390×844 capture keeps player resources above the 44 px utility rail, the
boss bar above the thumb cluster, and BREAK inside the established HVY target.
The 1440×900 capture keeps the rail inside the compact upper-left resource
stack.

## Audio contract

Gameplay names five new event boundaries:

- `sunderRelease`
- `sunderHit`
- `resolveReady`
- `gracebreakRelease`
- `gracebreakHit`

Sunder and Gracebreak reuse the accepted heavy sample when ready, plus the
existing procedural fallback, spatial routing, room send, critical-voice
reservation, music ducking, phone transient, compressor, and limiter. The
Resolve-ready acknowledgement is two short tones. There is no new MP3, manifest
entry, request, loader worker, startup node, or continuous layer.

Release audio still occurs on the visible strike frame, preserving the v2.26
event-ownership rule.
