## What changed

The existing original Blender characters now have pelvis, knee and ankle pivots.
Travel drives their footstep cycle, with bent knees, ankle leveling, hip weight,
torso counter-rotation and arm follow-through. Idle breathing and head movement
continue between actions. Cuts, heavy attacks, boss windups/releases/recoveries,
tucked rolls, flask drinking, stagger and death receive continuous joint motion.

This is authored procedural/keyed rigid-joint animation, not mocap or scanned
humans. It improves motion on the existing models; perfect foot locking, hand
contact IK and physically simulated cloth are not implemented. The established
small gameplay camera remains unchanged.

Combat remains authoritative. Animation never moves a collider or awards a hit.
Damage, timings, difficulty, inputs, sound, saves, scoring, Classic presentation
and arena art are unchanged. Incidental motion respects reduced-motion settings.

## Local review and acceptance

Run `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run qa`. On Windows with installed Chrome,
run `qa/reliquary.cjs` with `GRACEFELL_GPU_QA=1`, then
`node qa/reliquary-live.cjs` against the fixed-loopback preview.
The latter exercises keyboard and real touch at 1280x800, 390x844 and 360x640.
Emulated phone viewports do not establish physical phone GPU performance.

`node qa/motion-capture.cjs` writes a 10-second, 24fps before/after sequence to
ignored `.artifacts/motion/`. The before source is the deployed URL; the candidate
source defaults to `http://127.0.0.1:8493/`. `--after-only` preserves the historical
before frames. The audition uses actual runtime models with pinned snapshots,
clearly labeled as magnified motion rather than gameplay footage. Do not reuse
the before command once production changes without recording the new baseline.

No CI, PR, production-release or physical-phone acceptance is claimed here.
