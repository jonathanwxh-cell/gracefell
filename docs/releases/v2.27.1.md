## Outcome

v2.27.1 fixes the presentation findings reproduced by three independent
gamer-agent production reviews of v2.27:

1. a 360×640 opening placed Malakar and the first CHARGE lane beneath compact
   utility/tutorial chrome;
2. a 390×844 FORSAKEN packet placed `OATH CHAIN` over `IRONBOUND`;
3. first-time Sunder discovery and partial/full Resolve feedback could be
   clearer.

The release changes camera/HUD composition and teaching only. Sunder,
Gracebreak, every combat number and priority, difficulty, score persistence,
save schema v7, audio, assets, weather, and touch hit targets remain unchanged.

## Implementation

### Protected 360×640 composition

`shortTouchCombat()` identifies touch fights at or below 680 CSS pixels high.
It is shared by the camera and tutorial geometry rather than inferred
separately at render call sites.

For that lane:

- `combatZoomTarget()` uses 0.88× of the viewport-fit base in a clean fight and
  widens to 0.84× under later-phase/hazard load;
- `combatCameraTargetY()` shifts from the established 25% player/boss midpoint
  share to 43%;
- `combatTutorialLayout()` places teaching at `height - 208`, between the boss
  HUD and action cluster;
- the generic touch reminder is suppressed while that tutorial owns the lane.

This changes only the camera transform and screen-space HUD. Malakar and the
player retain their authoritative world positions, distance, collision, AI,
and timing.

### Combined Oath-chain / IRONBOUND plate

On compact touch screens, an active FORSAKEN packet renders:

`OATH CHAIN n/m · IRONBOUND`

The separate right-aligned `IRONBOUND`/phase-pip label is withheld only while
the combined plate is active. It returns as soon as the chain cue ends.

### Earlier Sunder and stronger Break teaching

- The first touch MOVE card includes `ATK×2 → HVY SUNDER`.
- The four Resolve segments grow from 4px to 6px and use a stronger empty-state
  contrast.
- Full meter says `BREAK READY`.
- Touch HVY still becomes `BREAK`.
- Desktop's active hint says `HOLD K: BREAK` when Resolve is full.

No new permanent surface, touch verb, live-region message, saved field, or
audio event was added.

## Unchanged contracts

- Sunder requires two connected lights and retains roll priority.
- Gracebreak requires full Resolve plus a full charged heavy, spends on whiff,
  restores stamina only on contact, yields to Execute, and cannot bypass
  FORSAKEN.
- Oath timing and the Journey/Oath difficulty table are unchanged.
- Save schema remains v7; scores and bests remain path-partitioned.
- The 45-cue SFX manifest, three phase tracks, limiter, ducking, and audio
  lifecycle are unchanged.
- The Blender-authored cached arena, Canvas Malakar, phase masks, visual asset
  version, and fallback routes are unchanged.
- `touchLayout()`, `playerHudRect()`, and `combatUtilityTop()` retain their
  target and resource geometry.

## Known limits

- Browser automation certifies layout, controls, state, persistence, asset
  loading, and audio routing; it cannot certify subjective audio quality,
  haptic feel, hand fatigue, or a natural human Oath +2 clear.
- The expert review measured a shortest sampled Oath +2 phase-three follow-up
  around 218ms and considered it close to the readability margin but credible.
  No timing change was made without a reproducible fairness defect.
- The optional query-gated Three.js Malakar proof remains non-default.
- The built Three proof still triggers Vite's existing large-chunk warning.
- Browserslist data remains seven months old. Neither warning is introduced by
  this release.

## Rollback

Revert the v2.27.1 runtime merge, run the complete gate, deploy that reviewed
revert, restart `gracefell.service`, and rerun public QA. Save schema v7 and all
persisted values are unchanged, so rollback needs no data migration.
